Where we go

Four places, each worth a trip on its own.

Sarazm and Penjikent

Sogdian cities

Sarazm is a 5,500-year-old UNESCO site. Penjikent was a Sogdian city on the Silk Road.

Hissar Fortress

Near Dushanbe

An old fortress with madrasas and a caravanserai, an easy day trip.

Khujand and Istaravshan

Old Silk Road towns

Two of the oldest towns in Central Asia, with a fortress, bazaars and old mosques.

Wakhan forts

Pamir

Ruined forts like Yamchun and Kaakhka above the Panj river, on the old Silk Road.
